Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GenZ Pulse Hub ("we") publishes news on this website and on our YouTube, Instagram and Facebook channels.

What we collect

  • Website visitors: anonymous, cookie-free traffic statistics (pages viewed, country, device) to improve the site.
  • Advertisers: name, email, phone, brand details and ad content you submit when booking an ad, used only to run, bill and remind you about your ad. Payments are handled by Cashfree; we never see or store card or bank details.

Google / YouTube data

Our publishing app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to and read statistics of our own GenZ Pulse Hub channels. It does not access any other user's data. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Meta data

We use Meta's APIs only to publish to and read insights of our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate (Cloudflare hosting, Cashfree payments, email delivery).

Your choices
